Plan Your Holiday

Care, without the clinic

Safety and comfort, said plainly.

We design for ease and we name the limits. This page is not a medical guarantee, and a holiday is not a promise that nothing can go wrong.

How a journey is planned

Every outline starts with questions: who is travelling, how you like a morning to begin, how long you want to be away from the room, and what would make a day feel like a waste. The timetable is written after those answers, not before.

Driving and rail time are capped in the plan you approve. If a famous sight would break that cap, we offer it as a choice, not a surprise.

What we look at

These are standards of selection and communication. They are not a certificate, and they are not the same in every building or every country.

  1. 01Accommodation. Quiet, a comfortable bed, dinner that can be served at a civilised hour, and a true description of stairs or a lift.
  2. 02Transport. The vehicle or carriage, the length of the ride, and where you will be met. Step-free access is stated when it is real, and not implied when it is not.
  3. 03The day itself. Walking time, shade, seats, and a return that is not an afterthought.
  4. 04People. A named host on the ground and a planner you can call. They coordinate. They do not provide medical care.
  5. 05Food. Diets are passed on in writing. We also tell you what to reconfirm, because a kitchen can still miss a note.
  6. 06If something changes. Weather, a closure, or a tired afternoon. The plan says who decides, and how you hear about it.

If you want a travelling companion with a clinical role, tell us, and we will say whether that sits outside what we arrange.

We say so before you pay. Some forts, gardens, trains, and boats have steps that cannot be removed. You can skip them, see them from another point, or choose a different journey. We would rather lose a photograph than surprise you at the door.

You receive names and numbers before departure: the host with you, the planner at the house, and the hotel. Families at home can have the same note, if the travellers want them to.
